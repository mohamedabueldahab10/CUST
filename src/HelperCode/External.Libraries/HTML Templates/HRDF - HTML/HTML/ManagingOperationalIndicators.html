The number of those registered in Taqat (the National Labor Portal)

Major

Indicator Type

Description

The total number of employers registered in the job portal (TAQAT) in all ranges according to the classification of the size of the establishment (micro - small - medium - large - giant) in addition to the national classification of economic activities ISIC4, which includes 19 sectors without duplicating the commercial registration numbers (unique numbers).
